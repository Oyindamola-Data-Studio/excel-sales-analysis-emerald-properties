## 📊 Project 1 — Descriptive Analysis

### ❓ Business Problem
Management had no consolidated view of how 193 leads were 
distributed across sales executives, branches, gender, 
property types and inspection outcomes.

### 🎯 What I Did
- Summarized total leads by sales executive
- Broke down leads by gender, sales status and branch
- Analyzed distribution by property type
- Summarized inspection outcomes
- Built an Excel dashboard for management reporting

### 🛠️ Tools & Techniques
`Excel` `Pivot Tables` `Charts` `Dashboard Design`

---

## 📊 Project 2 — Conversion Rate Analysis

### ❓ Business Problem
Management could see total lead volume but had no visibility 
into conversion rates across teams, locations and property types.

### 🎯 What I Did
- Calculated overall lead-to-sale conversion rate
- Compared conversion rates by sales executive
- Analyzed conversion by gender, branch and property type
- Evaluated impact of inspection status on conversion
- Built a branch-level deep dive report

### 🛠️ Tools & Techniques
`Excel` `Conversion Rate Calculations` `Pivot Tables` `Comparative Dashboard`

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways
- Inspection status showed meaningful impact on conversion rates
- Conversion performance varied significantly across branches
- Built two dashboards communicating insights to non-technical management
